Tusche Pixel Symbols (TuschePixelSymbols.ttf)

Drawn for the Tusche pixel style on a 7x7 grid. Master: assets/pixel/symbols/
tusche-pixel-symbols.vectorcraft (VectorCraft). Some bitmaps follow YAMI's 7x7
icon set (check, close, arrows, send, gear, folder, trash, power, bolt, leaf,
gauge, lock, mic, copy, image, magnify):
